  changes. When an item is finished it moves to section 1 keeping its ID, and the gap it
  leaves is not refilled, so "2a-3 is done" always means the same item. New items take the
  next unused number in their section.

3. KNOWN BUGS
====================================================================================================

3-11    Olivia: an off-centre signal loses its start. AFC can only recognise a signal 300 Hz
        off-centre once enough content has been heard; the retune restarts acquisition, so a short
        transmission loses everything before that point (the reference recording keeps only "YZ K").
        Fix: after lock, decode back into the buffered audio without printing the noise before the
        signal. Older than the 3-10 fix. Size M.
3-12    J-Digi's Olivia is probably not standard Olivia. The transmitter, receiver and test
        generator share a simplified scheme: no per-block scrambling code, no bit interleaving
        across symbols, and a 64-character set. Olivia as specified (and as fldigi sends it) has all
        three, so J-Digi likely cannot exchange text with other Olivia stations. Confirm with
        fldigi, then implement the standard framing in both directions. Size L.
