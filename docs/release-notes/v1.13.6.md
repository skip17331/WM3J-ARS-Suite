## WM3J ARS Suite v1.13.6

Settings that used to be saved but did nothing now do something: the amplifier page, the rig's **Auto band-follow**, and **LoTW / eQSL auto-upload**. One setting that nothing could act on yet, **PSK Reporter upload**, is hidden until it can. Interface size also gains 85% and 95%, and its smaller sizes now come out right on Linux.

### New
- **85% and 95% interface sizes** (Setup > Appearance > Interface size), between 75% and 100%: the list is now 50%, 75%, 85%, 95%, 100%, 150% and 200%. As before, a new size takes effect the next time you start ARS Suite.
- **The amplifier settings are real** (J-Hub > Hardware > Amplifier > Behavior). They need a working `ampctld`; with none running they do nothing, as before.
  - **Follow rig band.** When the radio moves to another band, its frequency is sent to the amplifier, and sent again after the amplifier reconnects. An amplifier with no frequency input refuses it, and that is ignored.
  - **Operate / Standby.** The choice is sent to the amplifier when you change it, and again when the amplifier reconnects.
  - **Standby on TX inhibit.** A rig that is not connected counts as inhibited: the amplifier is put in Standby while it is, whatever Operate / Standby says, and goes back to your choice when the rig returns.
  - **Max power.** Forward power over this is flagged with a warning mark on the dashboard's amplifier card and on the Amplifier page, which also has a new **Forward** reading. It only warns; it does not change the amplifier.
- **Auto band-follow** (J-Hub > Hardware > Rig control) makes the **New QSO** frequency, band and mode follow the radio while it is connected. A field you are typing in is left alone, as is a QSO you are editing. When the radio is in a digital mode (PKT...), the mode stays as you typed it, since the radio cannot tell FT8 from PSK.
- **LoTW / eQSL auto-upload** (J-Hub > Data > Logbook & export). After you log a QSO, the QSOs not yet sent go to LoTW and eQSL, for whichever is switched on in the **Upload** section. A failed upload is tried again after the next QSO. It happens in the background, covers QSOs logged from the New QSO form, the J-Map card, WSJT-X auto-log and the web client, and does not touch ADIF imports.
  - **Your logbook gets two new columns** the first time this version opens it, to remember what has been sent. A copy is made first, next to the log, as `j-log.db.bak-before-upload-markers`. QSOs already in the log are marked as done, so switching this on does **not** send your whole history; send older QSOs from the Upload section as before.

### Fixed
- **On Linux, the interface sizes came out far too small.** The size was handed to the toolkit as an absolute scale, replacing the one it picks for your screen (for example 2.0 on a 4K display), so 75% there drew at about 37% of normal. The size is now applied on top of the screen's own scale, so 75% on that display is 1.5. Windows is unchanged.

### Changed
- The amplifier's status reads Hamlib's power states as they are defined: 4 is Operate, 2 is Standby, 1 is On and 0 is Off. The readout used to take 2 as Operate and 1 as Standby.
- With the rig disconnected, and **Standby on TX inhibit** on (its default), a connected amplifier is now put in Standby when ARS Suite starts. The setting is on by default, so this applies unless you turn it off.
- **PSK Reporter upload** is gone from Setup > Data: nothing sent anything, so the switch did nothing. It will come back when reporting is built.

### Not yet tried
- A real amplifier: everything above was run against a stand-in `ampctld`. The Operate and Standby values are Hamlib's as remembered, not checked against an amplifier, so confirm that Operate and Standby are not swapped on yours.
- A real LoTW or eQSL upload. eQSL's reply is judged only by whether it contains the word "result", so a rejection could be counted as sent. Watch the first few.
- A real radio: band-follow was run against a stand-in `rigctld`.
- macOS, and the interface sizes on Windows: the Linux fix was run on a 4K screen only.
