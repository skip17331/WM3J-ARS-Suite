## WM3J ARS Suite v1.12.12

A visual and polish release: more depth and colour, things that announce themselves when something new arrives, a calmer and more readable J-Map, and settings that stay put between runs.

### New
- **Look and feel.** Cards, drawers and the top-bar tiles have depth (a soft shadow and a lighter top edge); each module's top bar has its own colour underline and the active dock row glows in it; the stat values are larger; placeholder text in forms is readable on the dark schemes; the focus glow follows the colour scheme; a short fade when you switch screens.
- **Colour scheme button.** The sunburst at the bottom of the dock changes the colour scheme again. Awards, Settings (now a sliders icon) and the sunburst stay pinned at the bottom of the dock however tall the window is.
- **New things announce themselves.** A new DX spot slides into the J-Hub and J-Map lists with a glow; the SPOTS / SHOWN counts flash when they change; live "connected" lights breathe. **Setup > Appearance > Reduce effects** turns all of it off.
- **J-Map markers.**
  - The Azimuthal / Rectangular / Globe buttons moved to the top right of the map, like J-Sat's view buttons.
  - Spot dots glow in their band's colour and fade as they age; callsigns are small readable labels that no longer pile up on each other.
  - A **starburst** plays where a new spot lands, and a smaller one on the globe's edge for a spot on the far side. It is tuned to be noticeable without being loud.
  - Callsigns **fade from the map** after a while, sooner when the map is busy (about 75 seconds with 80 or more spots showing, up to 8 minutes with 10 or fewer).
  - Both are switches under MAP CONTROLS > Overlays (**Starburst on new spots**, **Fade old callsigns**).
- **Settings are remembered between runs:** J-Map projection, region, overlays and spot filters; the J-Sat view (Polar / Blue marble / Globe); which drawers are open.
- **3D globe** (J-Map and J-Sat) is lit from one side and shaded, with an atmosphere glow, so it reads as a ball.

### Fixed
- **Colour schemes and other settings reverting.** The hub and each app window kept their own copy of `config.json` and wrote the whole copy back on any change, so one process could undo another's settings. Each change is now merged into the file as it is on disk and written atomically.
- **Pin / Unpin buttons** now always show the real state (J-Map's right-hand PANELS column used to say "Pin" forever).
- **Side columns auto-close again.** A column whose first text field had keyboard focus (J-Map's left controls) never closed after the pointer left; now only recent typing keeps it open.
- **J-Hub dashboard with the PANELS column pinned.** The DX Cluster window no longer lies over the New QSO form: when there is not room for both side by side, the cluster drops below the form.
