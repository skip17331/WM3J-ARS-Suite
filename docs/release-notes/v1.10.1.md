## WM3J ARS Suite v1.10.1

Side panels now collapse by themselves, and rotor control shows one true heading everywhere.

### New
- **Auto-collapsing side panels.** The right column, J-Map's controls, J-Sat's satellite list and J-Learn's chapters fold
  to a thin strip and slide open while the mouse is over them. **Pin** keeps a panel open and is remembered; **Unpin**
  lets it collapse again. This replaces the collapse arrows.
- **Beam on the rectangular J-Map**, drawn as a great-circle path out to 15,000 km.

### Changed
- **One rotor heading.** Every rotor display and J-Map's beam now follow the rotor itself. With no rotor answering they
  show "—" and a status, with no compass needle, controls or beam.
- **J-Map's beam slider is gone**, because it drew a beam the antenna wasn't on. Turn the rotor from the Rotor control
  drawer.
- J-Hub uses the same Rotor control drawer as the other modules.

### Fixed
- **Rotor readings.** A position reply with no closing `RPRT` line no longer times out and marks the rotor offline.
- **J-Map view.** The projection and pinned controls survive a switch to another module.
- **Projection selector** no longer highlights Azimuthal while the map shows Rectangular.
- **DX Spots drawer** count stays current while J-Map is open.
