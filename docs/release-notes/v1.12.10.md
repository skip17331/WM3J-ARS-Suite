## WM3J ARS Suite v1.12.10

### Changed
- **Dock.** The Info module now sits at the bottom of the module list instead of the top.
- **3D globe (J-Map, J-Sat).** The globe is now lit from one side and shaded, with a faint sheen and a soft atmosphere glow at its edge, so it reads as a ball rather than a flat disc.

### Fixed
- **Colour scheme button.** The sunburst at the bottom of the dock changes the colour scheme again (the current module's own scheme, or the whole suite's on J-Hub pages). Settings, which the sunburst icon used to stand for, now has its own slider icon.
