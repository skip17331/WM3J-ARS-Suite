## WM3J ARS Suite v1.10.0

J-Map can now log a QSO without leaving the map, J-Hub offers a pick list of DX clusters, and J-Map scrolls and repaints
smoothly. The app opens maximized and no longer leaves a blank terminal window open.

### New
- **QSO form on J-Map.** Clicking a spot tunes the rig and pops up the station's details. **Log this** opens a QSO form
  filled in from the spot, and **Enter** saves it to the normal log. **+ QSO** opens a blank form. **Pin** moves the form
  into a drawer in the right column, where a spot click fills it directly. Contest logging stays in J-Log.
- **DX station info.** The spot pop-up and the J-Map drawer show country, frequency, band-plan segment, beam heading,
  distance, spotter, comment and whether the station is in your log.
- **DX cluster pick list.** J-Hub lists seven public clusters, or type your own `host:port`. One runs at a time, and the
  choice is remembered.
- **Collapsible right column.** Any module's right column folds to a thin strip and stays that way across modules.
- **ID timer in the top bar.** A small button in every top bar replaces the Station ID drawer.
- **J-Sat uplink, downlink and mode tiles**, along the bottom of the screen.
- **Data tiles scale with the window.** Top-bar and J-Sat tiles and their text shrink with the window.
- **Sun position in the space weather card:** sub-solar point, azimuth and elevation, sunrise and sunset.

### Changed
- The docked app and single-module windows open maximized. A launch file can set `"maximized": false`.
- The J-Map ticker shows live solar data and today's gray line instead of sample text.
- J-Map's legend and the text beside the sun are gone. The azimuthal note shows the live spot count.
- The expanded dock scrolls with the mouse wheel when it is taller than the window.
- Spot ages on J-Hub and J-Log refresh every 30 seconds unless a new spot arrives.

### Fixed
- **J-Map dropped rare DX.** Spots with prefixes missing from a short table never reached the map. J-Map now uses the
  full DXCC data, so "Shown" stays close to "Spots".
- **J-Map stutter.** The hub's repeated spot list repainted the map every second, and each repaint stalled the screen
  for up to half a second. Repaints now happen only on a real change and take about 25 ms.
- **Screen jump on J-Hub.** Hovering the dock no longer pushes the page off the top of the window.
- **Dock buttons blocked.** A collapsed J-Map or J-Learn panel no longer covers the dock.
- **Blank terminal window on Windows.** The launcher uses `javaw` and closes its own window.
- **Windows start-up warning** about a legacy database file name.
- **J-Sat polar plot** no longer cuts off its below-horizon count.
