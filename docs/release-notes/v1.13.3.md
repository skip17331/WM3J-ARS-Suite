## WM3J ARS Suite v1.13.3

A look-and-layout release. Every screen now opens with a **hero bar**, the J-Hub dashboard is laid out in rows around a compact, full-width New QSO card, and there are two new looks (**Modern** and **Cockpit**) to choose from in **Setup > Appearance**. The default stays **Classic**: nothing about the colours or the old styling changes until you pick another look. What does change for everyone, in every look, is described under "Changed".

### New
- **Hero bar on every screen.** J-Log (normal and contest), J-Map, J-Sat, J-Digi, J-Bridge, J-Vault, J-Learn, Info and Setup open with the same banner as the dashboard: the module's name and subtitle, its own figures as large readouts, and on every screen the rig's frequency and mode, the DX cluster status, the transmit light, the station-ID timer and a UTC clock. The readouts shrink to fit a narrow window rather than cutting values off.
- **Two more looks** in Setup > Appearance > Look: **Modern** (soft rounded cards, flat fields, pill keys) and **Cockpit** (a redesign: glowing readouts, band-coloured spots, a cyan Log QSO key, and a stat strip under the dashboard's hero banner). A look covers every screen and pop-up window; **Classic** puts everything back.
- **Cockpit insight strip.** Four live figures (QSOs today, QSOs in the log, spots on the air, needed spots) and a band-activity chart under the dashboard hero. It is a slim line by default, with each band shown as a colour block carrying its spot count and the band underneath; hover floats the full strip open over the page, **Pin** keeps it open.
- **A Cockpit colour scheme.** The deep navy palette is now an ordinary scheme, so the **theme button** in the dock works with the Cockpit look and cycles Dark, Light, Ocean, Forest, Amber, Rose, Nord and Cockpit; the look follows each.
- **Band colours on DX spots.** Every spot row has a stripe in its band's colour (the same colours as the band-activity chart); J-Map's spot list has them too in Cockpit.
- **Band plan named under the callsign.** The line under the callsign field now says which plan it is quoting, e.g. "20m · CW / data only · US FCC Part 97 band plan" or "IARU Region 1 band plan".

### Changed
- **The dashboard header is a hero banner** (callsign and station, live rig frequency, cluster status, a big UTC clock) in place of the "J-Hub > Dashboard" line, in every look. It stays fixed at the top while the cards scroll. The "10/10 modules enabled" readout is gone.
- **Cards stack in rows** by default: the New QSO card, then the DX Cluster, then the Recent log. **Setup > Appearance > Stack cards in rows** turns it off for the old side-by-side layout.
- **The DX Cluster is a fixed-height row**; a long spot list scrolls inside it instead of stretching the page.
- **The LOG TOOLS tray** sits beside the cards at the same top and height as the PANELS tray, so the hero banner runs the full width above both.
- **The New QSO card on the dashboard is a full-width, compact row** (three rows of fields instead of seven; power, date and time, the award references and the comment share a row, and the F-keys sit with Log QSO). J-Log's own form is unchanged.
- **J-Map's "Log this" pop-up and the QSO form it opens** now sit down the right edge of the map, clear of the globe, and move left of the PANELS tray when it is pinned open.

### Not yet tried
- Narrow windows (the rows wrap rather than clip, but this was checked at 1920 px only) and display scales other than 100%.
- The Forest, Amber, Rose and Nord schemes with the new looks, and the pop-up windows (awards, cluster console, translator, contest picker) in them.
- Logging a real QSO through the compact dashboard card, and the J-Map pop-up position on the rectangular map and at other window sizes.
