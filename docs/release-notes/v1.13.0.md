## WM3J ARS Suite v1.13.0

A milestone release. It gathers everything added through the 1.12.x line - about eighty new features since 1.12.0 - and includes the visual polish and the settings fixes from 1.12.12 (see its notes for the detail on the new look, the map markers and the starbursts). The notes below group it by area.

### Contest logging
- **A one-screen contest page** driven by the contest definitions: an entry bar built from the contest's fields, F-key macros, a contest clock, dupes asked first, edit and erase, entry validation, a NEW MULT banner and exchange memory, score tiles, a standard F-key set and field order for each major contest, per-contest layouts you can override with your own file, and Space to advance.
- **Contest specials:** the WAE QTC pane (edit or delete a saved series), QSO-party bonus stations and an in-state / out-of-state mode, the Field Day worksheet, and 2026 bonus values and caps.
- **Super Check Partial** on the contest page and in the normal log strip, and **call history** that fills the exchange and tags NEW MULT from what each station usually sends (it learns from your logs, ADIF imports, network QSOs and QSO-party counties).
- **Network mode:** several operators log one contest over the LAN. One station hosts, the others join (found automatically, everything after the handshake is encrypted), the host hands out serial numbers, and there are run-conflict warnings, per-operator figures, chat and a Run / S&P switch. *Tried on loopback only so far - not yet on two real computers.*
- **N1MM+-compatible UDP broadcast** of the contest log (contacts, lookups, radio info, spots), with an inbound QSY listener. Off by default.

### Awards and the log
- **An awards screen:** DXCC (band-by-band matrix), WAS, WAC, grids, IOTA, POTA, SOTA and zones, with your own award sheets (**My awards**), award levels (DXCC Challenge, 5-Band DXCC / WAS, mode endorsements, VUCC) and a printable sheet.
- **Confirmations:** QSL, LoTW and eQSL tracked per QSO, with downloads from LoTW and eQSL, and park / summit / island lookups.
- **Much better DXCC resolution** from the contest country files (`cty.dat`, refreshed weekly), CQ zones from a grid square, and entity-aware NEED flags on cluster spots.
- **A full log browser**, and **QSL label printing (beta)** for Brother, DYMO and Zebra printers. *Not yet tried on real printers.*

### Maps, satellites and propagation
- **J-Map:** a vector basemap with wheel zoom and drag pan, country names and state / province lines in the colour scheme's colours; a **3D globe**; ham-to-ham tracks; spot paths from the DX cluster; a beacon watch that hops bands and keeps a log, with an Auto QSY option; and a **band map** in the DX cluster card.
- **J-Sat:** a 3D globe, satellite icons, lit ground tracks and footprints for every satellite.
- **A propagation estimate** (MUF, LUF and band openings from the solar flux, sunspots and K index) in the Propagation drawer, a **Path now** line on the DX info card, markers on the map and spot lists, and a self-check against what the DX cluster actually hears.

### Info, learning and the rest
- **An Info page:** upcoming contests (from the WA7BNM Contest Calendar, credited) and DXpeditions, a news tile, and links.
- **J-Learn** has Back / Next buttons so reading on needs no trip to the chapter list.
- **Updates:** the suite tells you when a new version is out and, on Windows and Linux, installs it for you.
- **A phone-friendly page** served by the hub (off by default). *Not yet tried on a phone.*
- **The local CW skimmer** works again, with a switch in J-Hub and its spots in the shared list.
- Local copies of the licence, Code of Conduct and legal notes, and a beta notice on the J-Digi and J-Bridge pages.

### Fixed
- Colour schemes and other settings no longer revert (the hub and each window used to overwrite each other's copy of the settings file).
- Pin / Unpin buttons show the real state, side columns auto-close again, and the J-Hub dashboard no longer overlaps with the PANELS column pinned.
