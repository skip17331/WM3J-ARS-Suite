## WM3J ARS Suite v1.11.0

The Antenna Workshop is back in J-Learn, and a lot of values that were hardcoded now come from your station and your
equipment.

### New
- **Antenna Workshop in J-Learn.** A questionnaire that recommends antennas for your lot and goals, plus 27 calculators:
  dipole, inverted-V, fan, trapped, OCF and end-fed antennas, J-pole, Yagi, vertical and magnetic loop; trap and
  loading-coil design; and the formula tools (Ohm's law, SWR, feedline loss, RF exposure and more). Open it from the
  J-Learn sidebar, from the button on chapter 09 and 17 pages, or from J-Hub ▸ Hardware ▸ Antenna workshop.
- **DX Cluster window in J-Log**, normal and contest mode, the same window as on the J-Hub dashboard. It replaces the
  DX Cluster drawer in J-Log's right-hand panel.
- **Rotor panel** uses your beam presets from J-Hub ▸ Rotor control, and has a **Park** button.
- **Auto-log WSJT-X QSOs** (J-Hub ▸ Data, off by default) adds each QSO WSJT-X logs to the J-Log database.

### Changed
- **J-Hub status strips are live.** Rig, Rotor, Amplifier, Data and Station now show the real connection state,
  frequency, heading and endpoints, refreshed every second. The Station strip derives DXCC from your callsign. The
  Antenna switch page says plainly that switch control is not built in yet.
- **Your location, not someone else's.** Latitude and longitude come from J-Hub ▸ Station, or from your grid square when
  they are empty. There is no built-in default location or callsign any more; screens that need a location tell you to
  set one.
- **J-Log Skimmer drawer** lists what your local CW skimmer actually decodes (turn the skimmer on in J-Hub ▸ Data).
- **Dock dots** show whether each module is really running. J-Bridge's sequence clock follows the WSJT-X mode (FT8 15 s,
  FT4 7.5 s, and so on).
- Settings that now do something: WSJT-X UDP port, default TX power, rig poll interval, rotor park and dead-band,
  auto-track satellites, DX cluster band and mode filters, CSV/Cabrillo charset. Station elevation is used for J-Sat.
  Settings that still have no effect say "not applied yet".
- The duplicate TX delay field on the Rig page is gone; the PTT delay under Data ▸ Transmit is the one setting, and the
  `{TXDELAY}` macro tag follows it.

### Fixed
- **Side panels.** A folding panel now opens over the content instead of sliding underneath it (J-Learn chapters, J-Map
  controls), and after visiting J-Sat the right-hand panels in other modules open again.
