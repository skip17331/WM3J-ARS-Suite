## WM3J ARS Suite v1.13.7

A small one: the strip of live figures under the J-Hub dashboard's hero bar now shows in every dashboard look.

### New
- **The insight strip in every look.** Under the hero bar (station, rig, DX cluster, clock) the dashboard has a strip of live figures: **QSOs today**, **in log**, **spots on air**, **new ones** and a **band-activity** chart in the band colours. It collapses to one slim line (hover it to open it over the cards, or **Pin** it open). Until now it was built only in the **Cockpit** look; Classic, Instrument panel and Modern never showed it. It now appears in all of them, and Cockpit looks as it did.

### Not yet tried
- The strip in the Instrument panel and Modern looks: it was checked in Cockpit and Classic only.
