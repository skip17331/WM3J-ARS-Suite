## WM3J ARS Suite v1.12.3 (draft — not yet released)

Award tracking is back in J-Log, with confirmations, IOTA / POTA / SOTA references, and a much more accurate DXCC count.

### New
- **Awards screen.** J-Log's **Awards** drawer now lists every award with what is worked and what is confirmed, and
  **Open awards ⤢** opens a full screen with a tab for each:
  - **DXCC** — a band-by-band matrix (160 m to 70 cm) and CW / Phone / Digital columns, worked (`w`) and confirmed (`✓`)
    for every entity, with search and a filter for "worked, not confirmed". Totals per band and per mode are shown above it.
  - **WAS**, **WAC** — chip grids of what you have, what is confirmed and what is still needed.
  - **Grids** (4-character squares), **IOTA**, **POTA** (hunter), **SOTA** (chaser) — lists with first-worked date, last
    call, bands and confirmation.
  - **Zones** — CQ (WAZ) and ITU zones.
  - **Summary** — all awards on one page. POTA and SOTA, which have no fixed target, count towards the next round number.
- **Confirmations.** Every QSO now remembers whether a paper **QSL**, **LoTW** or **eQSL** has come back. Tick them in
  J-Log's new **Confirmed** boxes, or import the ADIF file you download from LoTW / eQSL: QSOs already in your log are
  marked confirmed (nothing is duplicated). The log browser shows them in a **CONF** column (Q, L, E).
- **IOTA, SOTA and POTA fields** on the J-Log entry form, for the *other* station's island, summit and park. They are
  saved with the QSO, appear in the log browser, can be searched, and are written to and read from ADIF
  (`IOTA`, `SOTA_REF`, `POTA_REF`, plus `CQZ`, `ITUZ`, `QSL_RCVD`, `LOTW_QSL_RCVD`, `EQSL_QSL_RCVD`) and to the CSV export.

### Fixed
- **DXCC entities are resolved much better.** The prefix table behind the entity lookup had gaps: calls such as VA3…, VY2…,
  M0…, 2E0…, CR3…, CS2…, HG…, 4Z…, JG… and most Chinese calls did not resolve at all. About 270 prefixes were added
  (Canada, the UK nations, Portugal with the Azores and Madeira, Hungary, Israel, France, Greece, Japan, China, Taiwan,
  Australia, Argentina, Chile, Mexico, the Philippines, Indonesia, India, Pakistan and others). In a 1,193-QSO test log the
  calls with no entity went from 48 to 1.
- **Different entities no longer share a DXCC number.** India and South Korea, Iran and Kazakhstan, Jordan and Lebanon,
  Sierra Leone and St Helena, and Anguilla and Western Kiribati each had the same number and were counted as one. Pakistan,
  India, Iran, Jordan, Sierra Leone and Anguilla now carry the correct ARRL numbers.
- **WAS counts the 50 states** (DC and Canadian provinces no longer count towards it).
- Editing a QSO keeps its references and confirmations.

### Notes
- Zones (WAZ / ITU) are not worked out from the callsign: they count only where the QSO has a CQ / ITU zone, which today
  comes from an ADIF import.
- The prefix table is still not a complete ARRL list. A call that resolves to no entity is counted in the Awards
  header ("N calls with no entity").
