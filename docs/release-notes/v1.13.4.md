## WM3J ARS Suite v1.13.4

A small J-Map release: an on/off **Session QSOs** switch that draws an arc from your station to every contact you log during the session.

### New
- **Session QSOs on J-Map.** Open J-Map's controls and turn on **Session QSOs** (it is off by default, and your choice is remembered). While it is on, a great-circle arc runs from your QTH to each QSO logged since ARS Suite started, on the azimuthal, rectangular and globe views: coloured by band, with a dot and the callsign at the far end, and the newest arc heavier. A live count ("6 QSOs this session") and a **Clear** button sit under the switch; Clear forgets the session's QSOs on the map and does not touch your log.
- Each station is placed from the grid square it was logged with, or, with no grid, from its callsign's prefix (the country's centre), so a QSO logged without a grid lands in the middle of its country. A callsign that cannot be placed is counted ("n not placed") but gets no arc.
- QSOs from the log forms (J-Log and the dashboard), the J-Map QSO form, WSJT-X auto-log, the phone client and the contest page all count. An ADIF import does not, and nothing is saved: the session starts empty on every launch.

### Not yet tried
- Logging a real QSO through each of the five paths above (each adds one line calling the same code, and the arcs were checked with sample QSOs on all three views).
- On the globe, an arc to a station on the far side of the earth arches off the surface and runs past the globe's edge, as the ham-to-ham tracks do; whether you would rather it stop at the horizon is open.
- A J-Map open in a separate process (loose mode) only sees QSOs logged in that same process.
