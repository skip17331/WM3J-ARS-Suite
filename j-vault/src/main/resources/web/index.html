📋 Getting started
Why bother? An up-to-date inventory matters in four scenarios:
  • Insurance / theft — claims need a list with serial numbers and values.
  • Estate / SK handoff — your family needs to know what you have, what it's worth, and who would buy it.
  • Sale or upgrade — purchase prices and dates set realistic asking prices.
  • Recall / firmware — serial numbers let you check whether a recall affects you.
1Add equipment
Click + Add Item for each radio, antenna, coax run, tuner, accessory. Type and a name are enough to start.
2Capture serials & values
Edit each item to add serial number, purchase date, and estimated value. Serial #s are the single most-asked-for field on insurance claims.
3Add first-call contacts
Friends, club leaders, and dealers your family should phone first. Lower priority number = called first.
4Generate the estate PDF
Click Estate Document below to print a real PDF for the family-paperwork folder. Refresh annually.
