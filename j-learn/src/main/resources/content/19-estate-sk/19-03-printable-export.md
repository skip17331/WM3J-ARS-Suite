# Printable Export

> **Advanced callout convention:** sections or paragraphs intended for Extra-class / engineering depth are marked with a blockquote starting `> ⚙️ **Advanced —**`. Simple-mode renderers can hide these; advanced-mode renderers show them inline.

This section is the **template** for the document your family will actually read. Everything before this (§19-00, 19-01, 19-02) was for you, the operator, gathering information. This section is the form you fill in with that information, then export to PDF and store somewhere safe.

The intent is that a family member can take this single document and have everything they need: who to call first, what each item is worth, where to sell, what to absolutely not sell as junk, and how to do it gently and at their own pace.

## How to use this template

1. **Read through the whole template** below.
2. **Copy it into a word processor** (Google Docs, Microsoft Word, Pages, or print it from this page and write in margins).
3. **Replace all `[BRACKETED]` placeholders** with your specifics — your friends' names, your equipment, your values.
4. **Personalize the cover letter** — make it sound like you.
5. **Attach your inventory printout** (from §18) as the appendix.
6. **Save as PDF** + print one copy + store in cloud + tell your family where to find it.

The template below is what the document looks like when complete. Sections written to your family are in plain language; instructions to *you* (in italics) won't appear in the final document.

## TEMPLATE — Estate Handoff Document

### COVER PAGE

```
       For my family, when I'm not at the radio anymore.

This document is your guide to the amateur radio equipment in
the [shack/basement/garage/closet]. You don't need to know any-
thing about radios to use it. Take your time, work through the
list at whatever pace feels right, and call the people I've
listed when you need help.

I'm sorry I'm not there to walk you through this in person.
The next pages should be enough to get you most of the way.

Don't let anyone — especially someone who shows up unexpectedly
or calls you out of the blue — pressure you into selling any-
thing quickly or for less than what's listed. The real dealers
and friends listed here will treat you fairly. Anyone else, hang
up the phone.

I love you all. 73 (that means "best regards" in radio).

       — [Your name and callsign]
       [Date last updated: YYYY-MM-DD]
```

*Operator note: Personalize the cover. Make it sound like you. Add specific endearments, references to family, whatever feels right. The tone should match how you actually talk.*

```
Here's what to do, in order:

MONTH 2-3
7. For remaining items, decide:
   - Sell to a dealer (HRO, R&L, etc.) — fast, simpler.

   - Keep for sentimental reasons — completely OK.

MONTH 3-6
9. Execute the sale path you chose for each item. The dealers

- A few keepsakes (if you wanted them).
- Friends who got items they cared about.
- A reasonable amount of money from the sales.
- A station that's been handled with care.
```

## Storing the document

After you've personalized this template:

- **Save as PDF** (one file, all pages).
- **Cloud storage** with strong access control: Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud. Share access with at least one family member or trusted friend who knows it exists.
- **Print one paper copy** for a safe deposit box, fireproof safe, or filing cabinet alongside other estate documents.
- **Tell two people** where to find it: spouse + adult child, or two friends who know each other.
- **Annual review**: the first weekend of every year, open the document, update prices, add new gear, remove sold gear.

## Updating the document

Things that should trigger an update:

- New equipment purchase or major sale.
- Friend on the list moved or changed contact info.
- Local club leadership change.
- Price ranges shifted significantly (annual market check).
- Major life event (move, retirement, health change).

Set a calendar reminder: **first weekend of January, review the document**. Takes 30-60 minutes; saves your family hours of confusion later.
